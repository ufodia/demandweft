## Defensible scope

Describe Demandweft as a workflow that joins:

1. An evidenced customer decision barrier.
2. A usable proof mechanism with a truthful unsuitable or unknown outcome.
3. A distinct customer-value and business-outcome experiment.
4. Optional propagation justified by the next recipient's own benefit.
5. Economic limits and a decision rule committed before results are known.

## Closest primary sources

### 1. Free tools as marketing

[Corey Haines: free-tools / Free Tool Strategy](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/blob/main/skills/free-tools/SKILL.md)

- **Observed scope:** Select and evaluate useful calculators, generators, analyzers, and other tools as acquisition channels; connect them to the main product and consider investment, sharing, and measurement.
- **Boundary:** A free useful tool is an existing marketing mechanism. Demandweft adds an explicit buyer uncertainty, a delivered instrument, separate customer and commercial outcomes, and optional recipient value as one contract.
- **Avoid claiming:** First skill to build free tools, provide value before purchase, or connect utility to acquisition.

### 2. Evidence and low-cost demand experiments

[highstat: product-opportunity](https://github.com/highstat/product-opportunity)

- **Observed scope:** Find reachable buyer segments, evaluate alternatives and constraints, select a cheap demand experiment, precommit outcome rules, preserve negative evidence, and decide whether to validate, revise, or stop. The former `sprensis/product-opportunity` address redirected here when checked.
- **Boundary:** Rigorous demand validation and conservative learning already exist in a Codex skill. Demandweft focuses on building an intervention within a customer's buying decision, then testing both its usefulness and business result.
- **Avoid claiming:** First use of preregistration, evidence ledgers, economic gates, or an inconclusive result.

### 3. Buyer proof and positioning gaps

[Dan Stinga: buyer-proof-gap, inspected commit](https://github.com/DanStinga/peak-ai-buyer-proof-gap/blob/8ff4e17777f45329612c1bc69efcd0b5e4bfddbd/.agents/skills/buyer-proof-gap/SKILL.md)

- **Observed scope:** Apply declared buyer criteria in a constrained skeptical-buyer simulation, compare two incumbents' official proof, and produce a sourced positioning hypothesis or abstain.
- **Boundary:** Buyer objections and proof gaps are established starting points. Demandweft produces something a real customer can use; simulated reactions may inform hypotheses but cannot validate that intervention.
- **Avoid claiming:** First skeptical-buyer method or first evidence-backed competitive positioning skill.

### 4. Shareable and remixable interactive experiences

[remix: Creation Skill](https://remix4me.com/skills/remix--remix-creation-skill/info)

- **Observed scope:** Create mobile HTML experiences for a platform, verify them, embed them, and support remixing and controlled premium capabilities.
- **Boundary:** Interactive outputs, remixing, and distribution are not new. A portable result is one optional Demandweft mechanism; it must preserve the source behavior and privacy boundary and demonstrate recipient value.
- **Avoid claiming:** First mini-app skill, first interactive sharing system, or guaranteed viral growth. A server-backed experience is not a fully offline portable application.

### 5. Diagnose decision friction before persuasion

[AAJ Marketing Skills: Marketing Psychology](https://skills.aajconsult.com/skills/aaj-marketing-psychology)

- **Observed scope:** Identify the buyer's decision friction, select relevant behavioral principles, distinguish ethical persuasion from manipulation, and propose what to test.
- **Boundary:** Friction-first diagnosis is an existing approach. Demandweft's proposed contribution is carrying that diagnosis through an actual useful proof instrument, separated outcomes, optional transfer, and bounded commercial learning.
- **Avoid claiming:** First use of decision psychology, ethical persuasion, or customer uncertainty in marketing.
