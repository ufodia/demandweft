# Demandweft learning record

Complete the plan before exposure. Preserve the original plan when recording results. Use `unknown` or `not measured` when evidence is absent. This template contains no customer observations.

## Identity and scope

- Record ID / version: not recorded.
- Created / last updated: not recorded.
- Product, owner, and usable artifact: not recorded.
- Audience, buying situation, and reachable channel: not recorded.
- Authorized actions, maximum cash, operator time, and duration: not recorded.

## Buying question and evidence

- Customer's unresolved question: not recorded.
- Barrier status (`hypothesis` or `supported`): hypothesis until supported.
- Why this question matters: not recorded.

| Evidence ID | Observed, user-provided, inferred, or synthetic | Source and date | Scope and finding | Counterevidence or limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Experience contract

- Selected mechanism and promised useful job: not recorded.
- Real implementation or delivery method: not recorded.
- Appropriate / inappropriate / insufficient-information conditions: not recorded.
- Exact public claims and their evidence IDs: not recorded.
- Proposed distribution destination and invitation: not recorded.
- Propagation mode: none unless recipient benefit is established.
- Sender benefit, recipient benefit, and permitted shared fields: not recorded.

## Plan committed before results

- Hypothesis and one primary business outcome: not recorded.
- Assignment method, observation unit, denominator, eligibility, and exclusions: not recorded.
- Comparison, allocation, and attribution limitations: not recorded.
- Minimum worthwhile effect and economic assumptions: not recorded.
- Planned sample, stopping rule, calendar horizon, and outcome-maturity window: not recorded.
- Data-quality, customer-benefit, trust, cost, and capacity guardrails: not recorded.
- Conditions for a bounded follow-up, revision/stop, or inconclusive result: not recorded.
- Missing information and how it will be obtained: not recorded.

## Delivery and verification

- Built artifact and actual implementation status: not recorded.
- Ordinary, non-fit, invalid-input, and relevant privacy/cost checks: not recorded.
- What was executed, where, and with what result: not recorded.
- Mocked integrations and unverified behavior: not recorded.
- Published or exposed to customers: not recorded; preparation is not publication.
- Data kind, provenance, observation dates, missing records, and exclusions: not recorded.

## Interpretation and next decision

- What the evidence supports, with scope: not recorded.
- What remains unknown, including delayed outcomes: not recorded.
- Economics, operator time, capacity, and unmodeled costs: not recorded.
- Negative results and alternative explanations: not recorded.
- Decision and bounded next action: not recorded.
- Changed assumptions / next version / authorization needed: not recorded.

Keep unfavorable and inconclusive results. Do not overwrite a failed plan with a new success definition or label a demonstration as customer evidence.
